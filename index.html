Field notes

Three stories, including a setback

Toronto Region Board of Trade
Far exceeded expectations

From talking to doing

Context
Organizations in the smart cities space were talking a lot and doing little.
Action
Founded the Smart Cities Working Group and connected it to the Board's advocacy strategy, so the conversations had somewhere to go.
Result
It became a major draw for new members from tech and professional services, and grew so fast it outgrew the space available for meetings.

Tie a forum to a real goal and the talk turns into action.

Communitech
143 introductions · $3M pipeline

Future of Cities, from a laptop

Context
No program existed for connecting corporate buyers with city-focused founders.
Action
Built it alone: the program, the partner offer, and a way to track who met whom.
Result
143 introductions and a $3M pipeline across six buyer personas, and an invitation to help build the National Security program.

When introductions are tracked and followed up, they become pipeline.

The Logic
The one that didn't hold

A sales pipeline that depended on events

Context
Joined as Head of Sales in 2019 with no corporate sales function in place.
Action
Built the first corporate sales pipeline and CRM.
Result
Most corporate revenue came from events. When COVID-19 suspended them, the role was eliminated.

Revenue that depends on one format is fragile, so I now build partner models that can survive a change of format.

How I work

Map, convene, mobilize, report up

The same four steps have run through every sector I've worked in.

Map

Chart the associations, institutions, government, investors and startups, and find where an intervention would matter.

Convene

Bring the right people into one room around a shared forum that didn't exist before.

Mobilize

Turn the room into programs, partners and introductions that lead to real work.

Report up

Give leadership a landscape, a strategy and a case for action they can approve.
